How it works

  • Normalises phone numbers to digits with the country code stripped, emails to lowercase, and names to a clean lowercase form.
  • Groups duplicates by exact phone or exact email, plus fuzzy name and ZIP matches scored with a documented Levenshtein and token method.
  • Nothing is auto-merged: every cluster shows its confidence, and clusters below the review threshold are flagged for a human.
  • Routes each lead with an editable rules config: state eligibility first, then debt band, then partner priority.
  • Builds a duplicate-credit ledger (vendor, lead, duplicate-of, credit status pending) and funnel counts by source. Sample leads are synthetic only.
